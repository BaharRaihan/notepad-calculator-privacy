Privacy Policy

Notepad Calculator · Developer: Bahar Uddin Raihan · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Your notes stay on your device. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob.

Your notes

All notes and calculations are stored only on your device. The app has no account, no login and no server of ours, and we never receive your notes.

Ads

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive your notes through this. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings > Google > Ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.

Sharing and backup

"Share" and "Back up" open Android's own share sheet, and you choose where the content goes. A backup file contains all your notes, so keep it safe.

App lock (optional)

If you turn it on, the app uses Android's device authentication (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern). The app never sees or stores your biometric data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all your notes. Without a backup they cannot be recovered.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
