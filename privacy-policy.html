Privacy Policy

Notepad Calculator · Developer: Bahar Uddin Raihan · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Your notes stay on your device. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob.

Your notes

All notes and calculations are stored only on your device. The app has no account, no login and no server of ours, and we never receive your notes.

Ads

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive your notes through this. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

Where the law requires it, the app first shows a consent message from Google (Google User Messaging Platform): in the European Economic Area and the UK (GDPR), and in some US states (for example California). There you can accept or decline personalised ads and manage your choices. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

What we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell personal information. The only data that leaves your device is what the Google AdMob ads library sends to Google to show ads, as described above. We do not use analytics or any advertising service other than Google AdMob. The app may ask you to rate it through Google Play's own in-app review dialog, which is run by Google Play.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights over personal data that Google processes for ads, such as access, deletion and opting out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising. Use the consent message in the app, your Android ad settings, or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, there is nothing further for us to delete.

Sharing and backup

"Share" and "Back up" open Android's own share sheet, and you choose where the content goes. A backup file contains all your notes, so keep it safe.

App lock (optional)

If you turn it on, the app uses Android's device authentication (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern). The app never sees or stores your biometric data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all your notes. Without a backup they cannot be recovered.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
